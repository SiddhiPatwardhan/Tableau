# 🚗 Electric Vehicles Data Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This Tableau dashboard presents an in-depth analysis of electric vehicles (EVs) across various dimensions such as model, manufacturer, year, state, and eligibility status. It helps visualize trends, patterns, and key insights that aid in understanding the current state and growth of electric vehicle adoption.

---

## 📌 Key Features

### 🔋 Avg Electric Range
Displays the average electric range of all electric vehicles in the dataset, providing an overview of how far EVs can typically travel on a single charge.

### 🚘 Total Vehicles Overview
Shows the total number of electric vehicles in the dataset, including a breakdown by vehicle type:
- Battery Electric Vehicles (BEV)
- Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEV)

Also includes the percentage distribution of each type.

### 📈 Total Vehicles by Model Year
Visualizes the growth trend of EV adoption over the years. The area chart highlights how the number of vehicles has changed from early model years to recent ones.

### 🗺️ Total Vehicles by State
A map-based visualization showing the distribution of electric vehicles across different U.S. states. Helps identify geographic hotspots for EV adoption.

### 🏭 Top 10 Vehicles by Make
Lists the top 10 vehicle manufacturers based on the number of vehicles in the dataset. Provides insights into which companies lead the EV market.

### ✅ Total Vehicles by CAFV Eligibility
A donut chart showing how many vehicles are eligible for Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle (CAFV) incentives or programs. Helps assess policy impact and adoption.

### 🚗 Total Vehicles by Model
Displays the top electric vehicle models along with their manufacturers and EV types. Shows how many vehicles each model contributes to the total and its percentage share.

---

## 🧩 Filters Available

The dashboard includes several filters to enable dynamic exploration:
- **CAFV Eligibility** – Filter vehicles by eligibility status.
- **EV Type** – Filter based on vehicle type (BEV or PHEV).
- **Model** – Filter by specific vehicle models.
- **State** – Filter data based on U.S. state.

---

## 🛠 Technology Used

- **Tableau** – For creating interactive data visualizations and dashboards.
- **Electric Vehicle Dataset** – Includes information such as make, model, EV type, range, manufacturing year, eligibility, and state-level distribution.
